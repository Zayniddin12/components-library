# Cleaning up the template

## Keeping things simple

To keep it focused, the examples leave out all of the logic, the state, props, computed props, watchers, and everything else. Only the template is included.

This means that some of the ways we refactor are less than ideal.

Often when moving a piece of template into it's own component, we'll move the computed props or state associated with it as well. But since we're ignoring that, we don't move them.

Don't worry though, we'll get there.

In this documentation, we'll go over strategies and tactics for refactoring and cleaning up the state and logic of your components.
